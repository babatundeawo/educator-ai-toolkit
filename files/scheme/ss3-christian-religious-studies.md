```
WEEK TOPIC                   CONTENT BREAKDOWN
1    Civic                   Meaning of civic responsibilities; examples (obedience to laws,
     Responsibilities        payment of taxes, voting, community service); importance of civic
                             responsibilities in nation building; biblical teachings on civic duties
                             (Romans 13:1–7).
2       HIV/AIDS I           Meaning of HIV and AIDS; causes and modes of transmission;
                             biblical perspectives on sexual purity; consequences of immoral
                             living; preventive measures.
3       HIV/AIDS II          Myths and misconceptions about HIV/AIDS; effects on individuals,
                             families, and society; role of Christians in showing care and
                             compassion; biblical support for healthy living.
4       Independence and     Meaning of independence; self-reliance and personal responsibility;
        Self-Actualization   ways of achieving self-actualization; biblical examples of self-reliant
                             individuals; importance of depending on God.
5       Midterm              —
        Examination
6       Dignity of Labour    Meaning of labour; biblical view on work (Genesis 2:15, Proverbs
                             22:29); importance of dignity in labour; consequences of idleness;
                             relevance of honest work in contemporary society.
7       Midterm Break        —
8       Christian Attitude   Meaning of corruption; forms of corruption (bribery, fraud,
        to Corruption        nepotism, dishonesty); effects of corruption on individuals and
                             society; biblical condemnation of corruption (Amos 5:12, Micah
                             6:11, Proverbs 17:23); Christian response to corruption.
9       Forgiveness          Meaning of forgiveness; biblical teachings on forgiveness (Matthew
                             18:21–22, Luke 15:11–32, Colossians 3:13); importance of
                             forgiveness in Christian living; benefits of forgiveness to individuals
                             and communities.
10      Perseverance,        Meaning of perseverance; biblical examples (Job, Paul, Joseph);
        Wealth and Poverty   wealth—meaning, acquisition, and biblical guidelines (1 Timothy
                             6:6–10); poverty—causes and biblical response (Acts 4:32–35,
                             Proverbs 19:17).
11      Revision             General revision of all topics covered.
12      Examination          —
13      Closing              —
```
