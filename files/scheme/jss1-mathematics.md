# JSS1 (Junior Secondary One) — Mathematics

*Full-session scheme of work (NERDC), extracted from the complete document.*

## First Term

*(Source heading: JSS 1 MATHEMATICS SCHEME OF WORK)*

```
Week Topic                          Content

1     Whole Numbers                 Place value, ordering, and properties of whole numbers.

2     LCM (Least Common Multiple)   Finding LCM using multiples and prime factorization.

3     HCF (Highest Common Factor)   Finding HCF using factorization methods.

4     Counting in Base 2            Introduction to the binary number system.

5     Midterm Exams                 –

6     Conversion of Numbers         Conversion of Base 10 numerals to binary numbers (step-
                                    by-step).

7     Midterm Break                 –

8     Fractions I                   Types of fractions (proper, improper, mixed),
                                    simplification.

9     Fractions II                  Equivalent fractions, comparison of fractions.

10    Basic Operations on Whole     Addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
      Numbers

11–   Revision & Exams              –
```

```

```

## Third Term

*(Source heading: JSS 1 MATHEMATICS SCHEME OF WORK)*

```
Week Topic                      Content

10    Everyday Statistics III   Data presentation: tables, bar charts, pie charts.
```
