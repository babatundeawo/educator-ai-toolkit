## First Term

*(Source heading: JSS1 FRENCH SCHEME OF WORK)*

```
Week Topic                        Content
1     Words used in the           Classroom instructions and expressions (sit down,
      classroom                   stand up, open/close your book, etc.)

2     Saluer (Greeting)           Forms of greetings (formal and informal), responses to
                                  greetings, polite expressions

3     Se présenter (Introducing   Giving personal information: name, age, nationality,
      oneself)                    origin

4     Épeler un mot / Écrire un Alphabet, spelling names and common words, writing
      mot épelé                 dictated words

5     Midterm Examination         Assessment on weeks 1–4
6     Présenter quelqu’un         Introducing someone else: “Voici…”, “Il/Elle
                                  s’appelle…”, polite forms

7     Midterm Break               —
8     Décrire quelqu’un           Physical description (height, color, appearance) and
                                  character traits

9     Parler de ses loisirs       Talking about hobbies, leisure activities, likes and
                                  dislikes

10    Exprimer ses goûts et       Expressing likes/dislikes: “J’aime…”, “Je n’aime
      préférences                 pas…”, “Je préfère…”

11    Revision                    Revision of all topics taught in the term
12    Examination                 End of term exam
13    Closing                     Result processing and holiday
```
