```

                                Characteristics of each breed

                                Construction materials
5     Mid-Term Examination —
6     Poultry Equipment         Types (feeders, drinkers, brooders, laying nests, perches); Uses
                                and maintenance

      Practices                 Sanitation and record-keeping
9     Poultry Diseases and      Common diseases (Newcastle, Coccidiosis, Fowl Pox);
      Control                   Symptoms, prevention, and control
10    Classification of Feed    Meaning of feed; Ingredients (protein, carbohydrate, vitamins,
      Ingredients               minerals); Feed formulation
```
