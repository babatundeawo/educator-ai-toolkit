# SS1 (Senior Secondary One) — Visual Arts

*Full-session scheme of work (NERDC), extracted from the complete document.*

## First Term

*(Source heading: SS1 VISUAL ARTS SCHEME OF WORK)*

```
Week Topic                  Content
1     Introduction to       - Meaning of visual arts: consciously created through expression of
      Visual Arts I         skill or imagination - Visual arts as making skillful use of materials
                            for human use and pleasure - Classification of visual arts: Fine Arts
                            (Drawing, Painting, Sculpture); Applied Arts (Graphics, Textile,
                            Ceramics)
2     Introduction to       - Functions of visual arts: Utilitarian (teacups, clothes, posters);
      Visual Arts II        Aesthetic (painting, sculpture); Religious (idols, masks, divination
                            tray); Political (stools, symbols); Educational role of art
3     Visual Sense          - Dynamics of basic shapes in objects (e.g. cylinder in bottle) - Colour
                            reflections and mixtures - Uniqueness of surface likeness (texture) -
                            Nuances of colour mixture on objects
4     Professions and Job   - Professions: graphic designer, video editor, cartoonist, textile
      Opportunities in      designer, painter, sculptor, ceramicist, photographer, cultural officer,
      Visual Arts           medical illustrator, investigative illustrator
5     Midterm               —
      Examination
6     Drawing Lines         - Concept of lines - Types: vertical, horizontal, diagonal, wavy,
                            spiral, zig-zag - Qualities: thick, thin, heavy, light, rigid, flexible -
                            Lines as patterns in nature
7     Midterm Break         —
8     Drawing Nature and    - Woodland (trees without leaves) - Animal patterns (zebra,
      Man-Made Objects      skeletons, veins in leaves) - Lines as patterns in man-made objects
                            (railway lines, bridges, fences, steel frameworks)
9     Drawing and           - Eye and hand coordination - Imitative drawing - Shading
      Shading               techniques: cross-hatching, pointillism, blurring, hatching
10    Drawing Still Life    - Composition of inanimate objects - Perspective in object drawing -
      Objects               Texture and tone in natural objects (flowers, rocks, fruits, vegetables,
                            insects, fish)
11    Drawing the Human     - Life drawing composition - Proportion - Tonality
      Figure
12    Introduction to Art   - Analysis and evaluation of works of art - Elements and principles of
      Appreciation          design - Composition and harmony - Significance of pictorial
                            elements - Art terminologies: appliqué, balance, bas-relief,
                            calligraphy, cartoon, motif, silhouette, cire perdue

13   Revision,       —
     Examination &
     Closing
```

## Second Term

*(Source heading: SS1 VISUAL ARTS SCHEME OF WORK)*

```
Week Topic                   Content
1     Introduction to        - Block lettering and calligraphic lettering - Spacing, formation,
      Graphics I             streamlining, loops, curves, ascenders, descenders - Skill and
                             craftsmanship
2     Introduction to        - Elements of design: line, texture, colour, size, value, shape -
      Graphics II            Principles of design: balance, contrast, proportion, variety,
                             gradation, harmony
3     Forms of Graphic       - Types: newspapers, posters, handbills, invitation cards, book
      Advertisement I        covers
4     Forms of Graphic       - CD jackets, banners - Features: suitable design, legibility,
      Advertisement II       communication, technical proficiency, colour distribution,
                             harmony, symbolism
5     Midterm                —
      Examination
6     Introduction to        - Computer hardware: monitor, system unit, printer, keyboard,
      Computer Graphics      mouse - Computer software: instructions for operation (CorelDraw,
                             Harvard Graphics, Photoshop)
7     Midterm Break          —
8     Computer Graphics II - Basic mouse techniques: pointing, clicking, double-clicking,
                           dragging
9     Textile Designs I      - Motif: single unit of design - Repeat patterns: block, half drop,
                             mirror, ogee, brick, diamond
10    Textile Designs II     - Printed textile design - Printing techniques: block, lino cut,
                             woodcut, yarn cut, screen printing
11    Terminologies in 2-    - Terms: vanishing point, medium, chiaroscuro, foreshortening,
      Dimensional Art        aerial view, repeat motif, picture plane
12    Terminologies in 3-    - Terms: terra-cotta, sculpture in the round, greenware, leather hard,
      Dimensional Art        maquette, relief
13    Revision,              —
      Examination &
      Closing
```

      Modelling II            pliability, plasticity, porosity
3     Introduction to         - Ceramic tools and equipment - Methods of moulding: coiling,
      Modelling III           slabbing, pinching

      Modelling IV

      Examination

      of Nigerian Arts II
9     Historical Foundation - Benin art: bronze works, ivory carving - Owo terracotta - Tsoede
      of Nigerian Arts III  bronze

      of Nigerian Arts IV   Annangs - Alok monoliths (Ikom, Cross River State)
